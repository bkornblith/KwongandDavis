Our Story

Built on a belief

Davis & Kwong LLP was founded by two immigration attorneys who met while representing clients at the New York Legal Assistance Group — one of the nation's largest legal services providers. United by a shared belief that immigration law should be a force for equity, Brendan and Julia launched the firm to bring the same depth of public-interest experience to a wider community of clients.

The firm's practice spans the full breadth of US immigration law, with particular expertise in humanitarian cases, asylum proceedings, and removal defense. Every client is treated as a whole person, not a case number.

Practice Areas

How we can
help you

Our practice spans the full spectrum of US immigration law, with deep expertise in humanitarian protection and the cases that turn on getting the details right.

⚖️
Asylum & Humanitarian Protection

Representing individuals who have fled persecution based on race, religion, nationality, political opinion, or membership in a particular social group. We handle affirmative and defensive asylum cases with sensitivity and rigor.

🌏
Citizenship & Naturalization

Guiding lawful permanent residents through naturalization — eligibility review, the N-400 application, the civics and English examinations, and the oath ceremony. We also handle derivative citizenship claims.

👨‍👩‍👧
Family-Based Immigration

Petitions to reunite immediate relatives and qualifying family members with their US citizen or lawful permanent resident sponsors. We guide you through every step of the petition and adjustment process.

🛡️
Removal Defense & Appeals

Representation before immigration courts and the Board of Immigration Appeals. We fight to protect our clients from deportation and exhaust every available legal avenue for relief.

🌿
Green Cards & Adjustment

Guiding clients through employment-based, family-based, and diversity-based pathways to lawful permanent residence — including adjustment of status and consular processing.

📋
DACA, TPS & Special Visas

Renewals and applications for Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals, Temporary Protected Status, special immigrant juvenile status, U visas for crime victims, and T visas for trafficking survivors.

Brendan has spent his career building immigration legal services where they were most needed. Julia is a seasoned immigration attorney with deep roots in public interest law.
